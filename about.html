About Ahmad

I’m an IT student based in Sydney who likes taking things apart to see how they work. That curiosity runs through web design, cyber security and networking, and the Linux, virtualisation and hardware projects I build in my spare time.

Quick profile

Sydney, NSW. Studying IT at Western Sydney University and building websites, game projects and home labs along the way.

How I work

I learn by building. I pick a problem, such as getting an operating system to boot or making a transit network feel realistic in a game, and keep testing until it works. When something breaks, I work through it step by step, read the error messages and write down what fixed it.

What I value

  • Understanding how things actually work
  • Secure, reliable setups
  • Responsive and accessible design
  • Patience when debugging

What I enjoy

  • Web and interface design
  • Cyber security and networking
  • Linux, virtual machines and hardware
  • Building games and simulators
